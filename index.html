01 · Method

How a tonne of straw becomes a credit

VM0044 credits only the organic carbon in biochar that goes into soil and stays there. Each step uses the PDD's pre-feasibility inputs.

  1. Input 22,300 t dry feedstock
  2. × 26.9% yield 6,000 t biochar produced
  3. − 1,500 t to fuel 4,500 t applied to soil
  4. × 80% Corg 3,600 t organic carbon
  5. × 0.89 permanence 3,204 t durable carbon
  6. × 44/12 C → CO₂ 11,748 tCO₂e gross
  7. − 15% project emissions 9,986 tCO₂e net credits
Formula Net credits = Msoil × Corg × PF × 44/12 × (1 − project emissions) − leakage − buffer

Leakage and any buffer are TBD in the PDD and set to zero here. The 1,500 t/yr fuel stream is produced but not credited.

    05 · Open items

    What has to be proven before validation

    From the PDD's Appendix 3. Each one can change the credit volume or block registration.

    • Volume

      Process methane

      Replace the 15% deduction with direct flue-gas measurement over a full carbonisation cycle.

    • Volume

      Corg and H:Corg

      Lab-test representative Darkhan biochar. These set carbon content and the permanence factor.

    • Volume

      Soil offtake

      Secure buyers and application sites for 4,500 t/yr. Only soil-applied biochar is credited.

    • Volume

      Feedstock supply

      Survey herd numbers, straw availability and farm-gate price. Evidence the 40% straw cap.

    • Eligibility

      Feedstock eligibility and leakage

      Show each stream is waste biomass with no material competing use.

    • Eligibility

      Additionality

      Build the VM0044 v1.2 investment analysis from verified CAPEX, OPEX and revenue.

    • Registration

      Dates, site and permits

      Project start, crediting period, coordinates, permit register and land rights.

    • Registration

      Stakeholders and carbon rights

      Consultation, FPIC, a grievance procedure, and JCM versus VCS for each tonne.
